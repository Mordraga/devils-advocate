What is this?

A live debate game. A cauldron picks a deeply unimportant question, like "Is a hot dog a sandwich?", and hands each contestant a side at random. You don't get to choose. You argue it anyway, and chat decides who did it best.

How a round goes

  1. 1

    The cauldron chooses

    A topic is drawn and a side is dealt to each contestant. Nobody sees it coming, including the contestants.

  2. 2

    Chat votes first

    Before a word is said, the audience votes on where they stand. That's the starting line.

  3. 3

    The debate

    Contestants argue the side they were handed, whatever they actually think.

  4. 4

    Chat votes again

    Whoever moved the crowd furthest toward their assigned side wins. Changing minds is the whole game.
